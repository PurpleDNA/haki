The idea

Justice happens in the open, under the baobab.

Across Africa, communities settled disputes under a great canopy tree, in full view, together. Haki's mark is that tree. The flat crown is shelter and a level judgement. The branches hold it up for everyone beneath. The broad trunk is steadiness: a fair price, fixed and agreed, that never grows behind your back.

The crown returns as the dot of the i in the wordmark, so the name and the tree read as one.
